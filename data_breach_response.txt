Data Breach Incident Response — Example Practice Protocol

Purpose: to give all staff a clear first-response process if patient data may have been exposed, lost, or accessed without authorisation.

Trigger examples: a lost practice laptop or phone, an email sent to the wrong recipient containing patient details, or a suspected unauthorised system access.

Process:
1. The staff member who discovers the incident reports it to the practice manager immediately — do not attempt to fix it alone first.
2. The practice manager records the time, what happened, and what data was involved.
3. The manager assesses within 4 hours whether the incident is notifiable and, if so, begins the formal notification process required under GDPR (72-hour reporting window).
4. Affected patients are informed if the breach is likely to result in a risk to their rights and freedoms.
5. A short internal review is held within one week to identify any process fix needed.

Escalation contact: practice manager; data protection officer if the practice has one designated.

Review: this example protocol would be reviewed annually in a real practice, or immediately after any real incident.
