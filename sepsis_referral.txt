Out-of-Hours Suspected Sepsis Referral — Example Practice Protocol

Purpose: to standardise the process for staff identifying a patient with possible sepsis outside normal clinic hours.

Trigger criteria (example): new confusion, fever above 38.3C with rapid heart rate, or a NEWS2 score of 5 or above recorded by any staff member.

Process:
1. The staff member who identifies the trigger criteria immediately informs the duty doctor — do not wait for the doctor to become free.
2. The duty doctor performs a rapid assessment within 10 minutes.
3. If sepsis is suspected, the duty doctor arranges emergency transfer following local ambulance protocol and documents the call time.
4. The practice manager is informed within 1 hour for incident logging purposes.
5. A safety-netting call to the patient or family is made within 24 hours by the duty doctor.

Escalation contact: duty doctor via practice mobile; if unreachable within 5 minutes, escalate to the on-call senior GP.

Review: this example protocol would be reviewed annually by the clinical lead in a real practice.
