New Patient Registration — Example Practice Protocol

Purpose: to standardise onboarding of a new patient so nothing is missed in the first contact.

Process:
1. Reception collects identity verification and registration form, either in person or via the online portal.
2. The practice assistant requests medical records transfer from the patient's previous practice within 2 working days of registration.
3. A new patient health check is offered within the first 6 weeks, not made mandatory, and documented as offered either way.
4. Any flagged long-term condition on the registration form triggers a proactive call from the practice nurse within 1 week.
5. The patient is given practice contact information, out-of-hours guidance, and online portal access details at registration.

Escalation contact: practice manager for any registration unable to be completed due to missing documentation.

Review: this example protocol would be reviewed annually in a real practice.
