Same-Day Urgent Appointment Triage — Example Practice Protocol

Purpose: to give reception and triage staff clear criteria for booking same-day appointments versus routine ones.

Same-day criteria (example): chest pain, breathing difficulty, suspected stroke symptoms, severe abdominal pain, a child under 2 with a fever above 39C, or any presentation the caller describes as an emergency.

Process:
1. Reception staff use the same-day criteria list to triage incoming calls — if in doubt, always escalate to the triage nurse rather than deciding alone.
2. The triage nurse calls back within 30 minutes for same-day flagged calls.
3. If the triage nurse assesses the situation as requiring emergency services, they advise the patient to call the national emergency number directly and inform the duty doctor.
4. All same-day bookings are logged with the trigger reason for audit purposes.

Escalation contact: triage nurse; duty doctor if the triage nurse is unavailable.

Review: this example protocol would be reviewed quarterly in a real practice based on triage outcome audits.
